Battle Demo Round 2 artwork notice — 2026-10-09

Pike and WEREDOG are visual reinterpretations of characters appearing in
Albert Odyssey. Their designs were referenced from local original-game
screenshots and redrawn with imagegen. The user's approved concept designs
were adapted into newly generated animation poses. These are not decoded
original-game animation frames. Other four fighters and the backdrop use
project-authored procedural drawings; sound effects are synthesized.

This local prototype includes only the newly generated artwork and project
code. Original screenshots, extracted game resources, disc images, BIOS,
and emulator save states are not included. Godot and its dependencies have
separate notices in GODOT-LICENSE.txt and GODOT-COPYRIGHT.txt.

Albert Odyssey names and original character designs remain associated with
their respective rights holders. This notice does not grant rights to the
original game or represent an official remake or endorsement. Project code
licensing must not be interpreted as licensing those underlying designs.

Image provenance and build-time asset hashes are recorded in the source
repository's art/concepts/round2, art/atlases/round2, and
battle-demo/data/asset-manifest.json. BUILD-INFO.json records delivery hashes.
